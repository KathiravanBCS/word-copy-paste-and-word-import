# complex/engagement-report

Everything at once, in the shape a real document actually arrives in: a title,
outline-numbered headings (`I.` / `1.1`), a bulleted list under a Heading 2, a
shaded table with a header row and right-aligned figures, inline bold and
italic inside list items, a hyperlink, and Word's blank spacer paragraphs
throughout.

This is the regression net. If a change breaks something subtle in the
interaction between lists, styles and tables, it shows up here before it shows
up in someone's paste.
